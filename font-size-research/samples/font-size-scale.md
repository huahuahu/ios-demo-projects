# Font Size Scale

| Role | Base Size | Weight | Intended Use |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Caption | 12 pt | Regular | Metadata and helper text |
| Body | 17 pt | Regular | Primary reading text |
| Headline | 20 pt | Semibold | Section or row emphasis |
| Title | 28 pt | Bold | Page-level headings |
| Large Title | 34 pt | Bold | First-screen emphasis |
